📋 Executive Summary

Company overview, role summary, and what success looks like.

🏢 Company Overview

We are building a brand-new Platform group with a strong foundational team already in place. The company operates a mission-critical platform across three tiers:

  • Cloud: Worldwide SaaS deployment
  • On-Premise: Gateways / MSC2
  • Edge Device Sensors: SDR / IoT tactical devices

The platform handles real-time sensor data ingestion and inference at massive scale, with a strict "You Build It, You Run It" culture.

🎯 Role Summary

Lead Technical Anchor — Key Technical Partner to the Principal Architect. You will be one of the main players in the group, driving ideation, design, implementation, maintenance, and monitoring of the distributed ecosystem.

We need a heavyweight engineer with battle scars from planning and designing distributed systems from scratch, who is still "hot on code" and deeply hands-on.

⚡ Key Responsibilities

  • Key Technical Partner: Work with the Principal and Software Architect to translate vision into production reality.
  • Architect Distributed Systems: Lead design, planning, and implementation of high-throughput distributed systems across Edge, On-Prem, and Cloud.
  • Lead by Example in Code: Write sharp, scalable, maintainable code using Node.js, Express, and NestJS. Set engineering standards.
  • End-to-End Ownership: Full lifecycle from architectural design to deployment and monitoring. Ensure high availability and observability.
  • Backend & Infrastructure: Design high-performance backend tasks, infrastructure, and BFF layers with REST and gRPC for real-time sensor data.

✅ What Success Looks Like

  • You've shipped a production-ready, multi-tenant distributed system that ingests and processes sensor data across all three tiers.
  • The platform is highly available, observable, and scales elastically.
  • You've set the engineering standard for the team — code quality, testing, and operational excellence.
  • You're the go-to person for architectural decisions and technical mentorship.
  • You've built strong partnership with the Principal Architect and the broader platform team.
